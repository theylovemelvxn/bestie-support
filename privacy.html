Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what information Bestie may process, why we use it, and the choices you have.

Last updated: October 3, 2026

1. About Bestie

Bestie is a beauty and wellness companion that can help you organize routines, understand beauty products, scan products, receive personalized guidance, save preferences and interact with an AI assistant.

This Privacy Policy applies to the Bestie mobile application and related services.

2. Information you provide

Depending on how you use Bestie, you may provide information such as:

  • Email address and account information
  • Beauty profile information
  • Skin, hair and routine preferences
  • Beauty goals
  • Products you save or add to a routine
  • Messages you send to Bestie
  • Information you choose to ask Bestie to remember
  • Routine and wellbeing check-in information

You control what you choose to share with Bestie.

3. AI conversations

When you communicate with Bestie, your message and relevant context may be processed by our backend and by artificial intelligence service providers in order to generate a response.

Relevant context may include information you previously provided, such as your beauty goals, routine or preferences, when necessary to personalize the response.

Bestie is intended for informational and lifestyle guidance. It is not a substitute for professional medical diagnosis or treatment.

4. Product scanner and photos

Bestie allows you to photograph beauty products so the app can identify the product, read its label, analyze ingredients and retrieve relevant product and safety information.

Product photos are resized and processed for identification. Image metadata such as EXIF information and GPS location is removed during processing.

Original scanner photos are not intended to be permanently stored by Bestie.

Bestie may retain the resulting product information, such as the identified brand, product name, ingredients, match confidence and product information you choose to save.

5. Apple Health

Apple Health integration is optional.

If you choose to connect Apple Health, Bestie may read the categories you explicitly authorize, which may include information such as sleep, activity, resting heart rate and heart rate variability.

Bestie uses this information only to provide contextual wellness and routine guidance.

Bestie does not sell Apple Health data and does not use Apple Health data for advertising or tracking.

Bestie does not write data to Apple Health.

You can change or revoke Bestie's Apple Health permissions through your iPhone settings.

6. Location and weather

Local weather features are optional.

If you enable them, Bestie may use an approximate location to request local weather information from a weather service so your routine can take environmental context into account.

Bestie does not need your continuous background location for this feature.

Location and weather history are not intended to be stored as a continuous location history.

You can disable location access at any time in iOS Settings.

7. Camera and photo library

Camera access is used when you choose to scan a beauty product.

Photo library access may be used when you choose an existing image instead of taking a new photo.

Bestie only accesses these features after permission is granted by you.

8. Notifications

If you enable notifications, Bestie may send reminders and other product or routine updates that you have permitted.

Bestie uses an Apple Push Notification service device token to deliver push notifications.

Notification content is designed to avoid exposing sensitive health information or private conversation content on your lock screen.

You can disable notifications at any time through Bestie or your iPhone settings.

9. Purchases and subscriptions

Bestie may offer paid subscriptions through Apple's App Store.

Apple processes your payment information. Bestie does not receive or store your complete payment card information.

Bestie uses subscription infrastructure providers such as RevenueCat to determine subscription status, entitlement level, renewals and related purchase events.

10. Service providers

Bestie relies on trusted service providers to operate parts of the service. Depending on the feature used, these may include:

  • Apple for App Store purchases, HealthKit and push notifications
  • Supabase for authentication and application data infrastructure
  • Railway for backend hosting
  • Anthropic for AI processing
  • RevenueCat for subscription management
  • Weather and product information providers when relevant to a requested feature

These providers may process information only as necessary to deliver their respective services, subject to their own terms and privacy obligations.

11. Product and safety information

Bestie's product scanner may retrieve product information from manufacturer pages, structured product databases, public regulatory sources and scientific sources.

Product formulations and regulatory information can change. Bestie therefore cannot guarantee that every external product record is complete or current at all times.

Safety information is provided for informational purposes and should not be interpreted as a medical diagnosis.

12. How we use information

We may use information to:

  • Operate and secure Bestie
  • Authenticate your account
  • Provide personalized responses
  • Remember preferences you choose to save
  • Create and manage routines
  • Identify products
  • Provide ingredient and product information
  • Manage subscriptions
  • Deliver notifications you enable
  • Prevent abuse and enforce usage limits
  • Diagnose technical issues and improve reliability

13. Tracking and advertising

Bestie does not sell your personal information to advertisers.

Bestie does not use Apple Health information for advertising or cross-app tracking.

If Bestie's advertising or tracking practices materially change in the future, this Privacy Policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated accordingly.

14. Data retention

Account and application data may be retained while your account remains active and as reasonably necessary to provide Bestie's services, maintain security and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Some temporary technical information may be retained for a shorter period for security, reliability and troubleshooting.

15. Account deletion

Bestie provides account deletion functionality.

When you request account deletion, Bestie will delete or de-identify account-associated information in accordance with applicable requirements, except where retention is legally required.

Some information maintained independently by Apple, such as App Store purchase history, is controlled by Apple and is not deleted by Bestie.

16. Security

We use technical and organizational measures intended to protect personal information, including authenticated access, encrypted network connections and access controls.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

17. Children

Bestie is not designed for children under the minimum age required to independently consent to the processing of personal data in their jurisdiction.

We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children where parental authorization would be legally required.

18. Your choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including rights to access, correct or delete certain data.

You can also control permissions directly from iOS, including:

  • Apple Health
  • Camera
  • Photo library
  • Location
  • Notifications

19. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Bestie evolves.

When changes are made, the date at the top of this page will be updated.
