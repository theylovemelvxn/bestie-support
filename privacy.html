8. Face Data and Skin Analysis Photos

Bestie allows a user to optionally take or select a photo of their face for cosmetic skin analysis and personalized beauty guidance.

Before the photo is transmitted, Bestie displays a consent notice explaining that the image will be securely sent for AI processing. The photo is used only to assess visible cosmetic skin characteristics such as dryness, hydration, shine, redness, blemishes, tone, texture, pores, dark spots, the under-eye area and visible sensitivity signs.

Bestie does not perform facial recognition, identity verification, facial geometry analysis, faceprint creation, facial embeddings, biometric identification, Face ID processing or TrueDepth processing. Bestie does not infer identity, age, ethnicity or other protected traits from the image.

The image is transmitted over HTTPS to Bestie's backend infrastructure hosted on Railway and then to Anthropic, our artificial intelligence service provider, solely to generate the requested cosmetic skin analysis.

Bestie does not store the raw facial image in its database, Supabase Storage or cloud storage. The image is processed in server memory only for the duration of the analysis request and is not written to Bestie's application logs.

Temporary copies of the image may remain in the application's local iOS cache and are managed by iOS. Bestie does not upload or reuse those cached copies for any other purpose.

The analysis result is not stored unless the user chooses to save it. If saved, Bestie may retain the structured text analysis, skin profile, observations, priorities and routine guidance until the user deletes the analysis or deletes their account.

Relevant context may be sent with the image to improve personalization, such as the user's skin profile, current routine, beauty goals and names of owned products. Apple Health data is not used for skin-photo analysis.

Skin Analysis is intended for cosmetic and wellness guidance only and does not provide medical diagnosis.
