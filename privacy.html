4. Product scanner and photos

Bestie allows you to photograph beauty-product packaging so the app can identify a product, read its label, review ingredients and retrieve relevant product and safety information.

Product-scanner images are resized and processed for identification. Image metadata such as EXIF information and GPS location is removed during processing.

Product-scanner images may be transmitted through Bestie's backend and to an artificial intelligence service provider, currently Anthropic, solely to identify the product and read packaging information.

Bestie does not store product-scanner images in its application database or cloud storage. Bestie may retain resulting product information such as the identified brand, product name, ingredients, match confidence and product information you choose to save.

Product-scanner images are not used for facial recognition, biometric identification or facial analysis.
